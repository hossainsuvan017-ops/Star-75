Quick help

Playback is not starting

Check your internet connection and use a playable MP4/HLS URL. Some hosts block playback from other sites.

Why does OTP not send?

The standalone preview uses a clearly labelled local demo sign-in. Real SMS OTP needs Firebase Authentication or another trusted auth provider.

How do I add my own episodes?

Open a title, choose Episodes, and use the episode selector. To publish your own catalog at scale, connect a database and video hosting service.
